How to use Last Done

Using the app, your data, and adding it to your home screen.

How to use

Type something you do on repeat (for example “Oil change” or “Water the plants”) and tap Add. Use Rename on a card if you want to change the title. When you do it again, tap Just now to record the current time, or use Different time and Add that time if it happened earlier. Open History to see past times—use × on one entry or Clear all to delete logs. Use Export occasionally to save a copy of your list somewhere safe.

Your data stays here

Everything is saved only on this phone or computer—nothing is uploaded or synced automatically. If you get a new device, use Export on the old one and Import on the new one to move your list.

Add to your home screen

In your phone’s browser, open this site, open the browser menu, then choose Add to Home screen, Install app, or similar. That puts Last Done on your home screen like a regular app.
