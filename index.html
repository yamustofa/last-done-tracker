Last Done

Track when you last did things—gas, bills, car wash, anything.

    No activities yet. Add one above to start tracking.

    How to use: Type something you do on repeat (for example “Oil change” or “Water the plants”) and tap Add. When you do it again, tap I did it now, or pick a date if you’re logging something from earlier. Open History to see past times. Use Export occasionally to save a copy of your list somewhere safe.

    Your data stays here: Everything is saved only on this phone or computer—nothing is uploaded or synced automatically. If you get a new device, use Export on the old one and Import on the new one to move your list.

    Add to your home screen: In your phone’s browser, open this site, open the browser menu, then choose Add to Home screen, Install app, or similar. That puts Last Done on your home screen like a regular app.
